# Acme Cloud — Billing, Refund & Cancellation Policy

> This policy governs billing, refunds, and cancellation for all Acme Cloud
> subscriptions. It forms part of the Master Subscription Agreement (MSA). Where
> this policy and a signed Order Form conflict, the Order Form controls.

## 1. Scope

1.1 This policy applies to all paid Acme Cloud subscription plans — **Starter**,
**Team**, and **Enterprise** — purchased through the Acme website, the in-product
billing page, or a signed Order Form.

1.2 It does **not** apply to:

- one-off professional services engagements, which are governed by the relevant
  Statement of Work (SOW);
- Acme Cloud Marketplace purchases from third-party vendors;
- trials, proofs of concept, and non-production sandbox accounts.

## 2. Billing

2.1 **Billing cycles.** Starter and Team plans are billed monthly or annually in
advance. Enterprise plans are billed annually in advance unless the Order Form
specifies otherwise.

2.2 **Currency.** Invoices are issued in USD by default. EUR and GBP billing is
available on annual plans on request.

2.3 **Taxes.** Prices exclude VAT, GST, and sales tax. Applicable taxes are added
at invoicing based on the billing address on file.

2.4 **Payment terms.** Card payments are charged on the invoice date. Invoiced
(net-terms) customers must pay within **30 days** of the invoice date. Accounts
more than 15 days overdue may be suspended after written notice.

## 3. Refund window

3.1 **Standard plans.** Customers on Starter or Team may request a full refund
within **14 calendar days** of the initial purchase or of any renewal charge.
Requests made after the 14-day window are not eligible for a refund, but will
stop all future renewals.

3.2 **Enterprise annual contracts.** Enterprise customers on an annual contract
have a **30-day** refund window measured from the contract start date.

3.3 **Partial periods.** Except as stated in 3.1 and 3.2, Acme does not provide
prorated refunds for partial billing periods.

## 4. How to request a refund

4.1 Email **billing@acme.example** from an address on the account, including the
account ID and the invoice number.

4.2 Approved refunds are returned to the **original payment method** within
**5–10 business days**. Card network processing may add a further 3–5 days.

4.3 If the original payment method is closed, Acme will issue the refund by bank
transfer once ownership of the destination account is verified.

## 5. Exceptions and non-refundable charges

The following are **non-refundable** under all circumstances:

- **Usage-based charges** — storage above the plan quota, egress/bandwidth,
  additional seats added mid-cycle, and overage on metered features.
- **Accounts terminated for cause** — a violation of the Acceptable Use Policy
  (AUP) or the MSA voids all refund eligibility.
- **Promotional credits and discounts** — these have no cash value and are never
  refunded or exchanged.
- **Third-party pass-through costs** already incurred by Acme on the customer's
  behalf (e.g. dedicated IP allocations, premium support add-ons already
  consumed).

## 6. Cancellation

6.1 **Self-service.** Starter and Team customers may cancel at any time from
**Settings → Billing → Cancel plan**. Access continues until the end of the
current paid period; the plan is not renewed.

6.2 **Enterprise.** Enterprise customers cancel by giving written notice to their
account manager at least **30 days** before the renewal date. Auto-renewal is the
default unless the Order Form says otherwise.

6.3 **Data retention after cancellation.** Customer content is retained for
**30 days** after the subscription ends, during which the account can be
reactivated with data intact. After 30 days the content is permanently deleted
and cannot be recovered. Backups containing the content are purged on a rolling
**35-day** cycle.

6.4 **Export.** Customers may export their data at any time before deletion via
the in-product export tool or the Data API.

## 7. Price changes

7.1 Acme may change list prices with **60 days'** notice. Price changes take
effect at the customer's next renewal. A customer who does not accept a price
increase may cancel under Section 6 before the renewal date without penalty.

## 8. Disputes

8.1 Billing disputes must be raised within **60 days** of the invoice date.
Undisputed portions of an invoice remain payable on the original terms.
