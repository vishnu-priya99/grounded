Billing, refunds & cancellation

This article explains, in plain terms, how billing works for Acme Cloud subscriptions, when you can get a refund, how SLA service credits are applied, and how to cancel. It summarises the Billing, Refund & Cancellation Policy (v3.2, effective 12 March 2026); where the two differ, the policy and your Order Form control.

Which plans this covers

It applies to all paid Acme Cloud plans — Starter, Team, and Enterprise — bought on the website, the in-product billing page, or a signed Order Form. It does not cover one-off professional services (governed by the Statement of Work), Marketplace purchases from third-party vendors, or trial and sandbox accounts.

How billing works

  • Starter and Team are billed monthly or annually in advance; Enterprise is billed annually in advance.
  • Invoices are issued in USD by default. EUR and GBP are available on annual plans on request.
  • Prices exclude VAT, GST and sales tax; applicable tax is added at invoicing.
  • Card payments are charged on the invoice date. Invoiced customers pay within 30 days. Accounts more than 15 days overdue may be suspended after written notice.

Refund window

You can request a full refund within 14 calendar days of your initial purchase or of any renewal charge on Starter or Team. Enterprise annual contracts have a 30-day refund window measured from the contract start date. After the window closes we can still stop future renewals, but the current charge is not refundable. We do not prorate partial billing periods.

What is never refunded

  • Usage-based charges — storage above your plan quota, egress/bandwidth, seats added mid-cycle, and metered overage.
  • Accounts terminated for a breach of the Acceptable Use Policy or the MSA.
  • Promotional credits and discounts (no cash value).
  • Third-party pass-through costs already incurred on your behalf, such as dedicated IP allocations.

How to request a refund

  1. Email billing@acme.example from an address on the account.
  2. Include your account ID and the invoice number.
  3. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method in 5–10 business days (card networks may add 3–5 days).

If the original card is closed, we refund by bank transfer once we have verified ownership of the destination account.

SLA service credits

Service credits are separate from refunds. If monthly API availability for a paid service falls below the 99.9% Service Level Objective, you can claim a credit against a future invoice:

Monthly availabilityService credit
99.0% – < 99.9%10% of the monthly fee for the affected service
95.0% – < 99.0%25% of the monthly fee
< 95.0%50% of the monthly fee

Claims must be filed within 30 days of the end of the affected month and include the incident dates and affected endpoints. Credits are capped at 50% of that month’s fee and cannot be exchanged for cash.

Example. During the 47-minute EMEA API incident on 9 March 2026, monthly availability for the EMEA region stayed above 99.9%, so that month did not by itself trigger a credit. Customers who were affected during a month that did breach the SLO should still file a claim — see the published incident history for exact windows.

Cancelling your subscription

  • Starter and Team: cancel any time from Settings → Billing → Cancel plan. Access continues to the end of the paid period; the plan does not renew.
  • Enterprise: give written notice to your account manager at least 30 days before the renewal date. Auto-renewal is the default unless your Order Form says otherwise.

What happens to your data

Customer content is retained for 30 days after the subscription ends, during which the account can be reactivated with data intact. After 30 days the content is permanently deleted; backups are purged on a rolling 35-day cycle. You can export at any time before deletion from the in-product export tool or the Data API.

Price changes

We give 60 days’ notice of any list-price change, which takes effect at your next renewal. If you do not accept an increase, you may cancel before the renewal date without penalty.

Billing disputes

Raise any billing dispute within 60 days of the invoice date. Undisputed portions of an invoice remain payable on the original terms.
